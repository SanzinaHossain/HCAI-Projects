Why Hybrid?

Pure uncertainty sampling (querying only the classifier's lowest-confidence examples) collapses at low query budgets because the resulting labeled pool is unrepresentative of the full confidence distribution, miscalibrating the deferral threshold. The hybrid strategy (50% uncertainty + 50% random queries) avoids this by keeping the labeled pool representative of the full distribution while still concentrating queries on the decision-relevant region.
