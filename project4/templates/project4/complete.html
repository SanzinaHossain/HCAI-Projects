i What do these agreement scores mean?

The score is a proportion from 0 to 1. For example, 0.75 means that model predicted 75% of your held-out choices in the same direction you actually chose. With only 8 validation choices from one person, this is a demonstration score, not a scientific conclusion.

i Which method “won”?

No scientific winner can be declared from one demonstration session. In the proposed real study, the analysis would compare participant-level predictive agreement, ease, confidence, and completion time across many participants, with paired statistical analysis, effect sizes, confidence intervals, and an order-effect check.
