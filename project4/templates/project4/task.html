i What does my answer tell the model?

If you choose Movie A over Movie B, the Bradley–Terry model receives one preference: A is preferred to B. It then adjusts the hidden preference weights so movies with features similar to A become more likely to receive a higher utility score.

i How do I move a movie?

You can drag a row to a new position, or use the ↑ Up and ↓ Down buttons. The numbered circles automatically update. This makes the ranking usable with a mouse, keyboard, or touch device.

i How does one ranking teach the model?

The Plackett–Luce model reads your order as a sequence: your #1 movie was preferred over all movies below it, then your #2 movie was preferred over everything still below it, and so on.
