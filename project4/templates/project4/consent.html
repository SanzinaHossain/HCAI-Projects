Before you begin

Study information and consent

This is a course demonstration of a proposed user study. You will make movie preference choices. There are no right or wrong answers: choose according to what you would rather watch.

What you will doTry both preference methods, answer short feedback questions, then complete final comparison choices.
Data in this demoYour progress and answers are kept in the temporary study session. This demonstration does not claim to be a completed real study.
Voluntary participationYou may stop at any time by leaving the page. You can also use “Main Home” and start a fresh session later.
i What will happen after I click Continue?
  1. You will complete the first randomly selected method.
  2. You will rate how easy it felt and how confident you are in your answers.
  3. You will complete the other method and rate it too.
  4. You will make 8 final two-movie choices that were kept separate from training.
  5. You will see a simple explanation of how well each learned model agreed with those final choices.
i Why is the order random?

If everyone always did the same method first, practice or tiredness could unfairly help one method. Randomly using pairwise-first or ranking-first reduces this order effect.
