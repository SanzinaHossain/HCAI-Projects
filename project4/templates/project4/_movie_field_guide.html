i What do the movie fields mean?
TitleThe name of the movie.
YearThe year the movie was released.
GenresCategories such as Action, Comedy, Drama, or Sci-Fi.
DurationApproximate running time in minutes.
Content ratingThe audience classification, such as PG, PG-13, or R.
IMDb scoreA public IMDb rating from 0 to 10. It is shown as movie information; you should still choose according to your own taste.
LanguageThe movie's listed language in the dataset.
